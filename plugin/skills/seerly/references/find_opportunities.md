# find_opportunities: Growth opportunities

Read-only.

Where the brand can grow, in one call: citation gaps (questions and topics where competitors are cited and the brand is not, how long they have been open, the suggested fix), critical topics, keywords to target split into fat-head and long-tail terms, Google Search Console opportunities and quick wins, and backlink opportunities (domains linking to competitors but not the brand). Use a gap's topicId with get_brief_inputs to plan content for it.

## Arguments

| Argument | Type | Required | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `project` | string | yes | Which Seerly project: its domain (e.g. acme.com), shortId or id, as listed by list_projects. |
